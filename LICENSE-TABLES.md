# License of the two POSS-I tables

The two tables in `targets/precovery/` are by Donal Hill. They come from his "POSS Transients Re-examined", version 1.4.0 (https://github.com/donalhill/poss-transients, doi:10.5281/zenodo.23549537). The files have been renamed; their contents are unchanged.

- `poss1_red_dates_hill2026.csv` is his `data/plate_date_corrections.csv`: the corrected dates of the POSS-I red plates.
- `poss1_red_times_hill2026.csv` is his `data/plate_time_posterior.csv`: the plate clock offsets and mid-exposure times, calibrated against asteroid trails.
